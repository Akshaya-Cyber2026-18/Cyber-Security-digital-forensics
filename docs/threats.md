# Common Cyber Security Threats

## 1. Phishing
Phishing is an attempt to trick users into revealing sensitive information through fake messages or websites.

## 2. Malware
Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information, or disrupt operations.

## 3. Ransomware
Ransomware can encrypt files and demand payment for restoring access.

## 4. Password Attacks
Attackers may attempt to obtain passwords using guessing, credential theft, or other techniques.

## 5. Social Engineering
Social engineering uses manipulation or deception to obtain information or access.

## Prevention

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Keep software updated.
- Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
- Maintain regular backups.
